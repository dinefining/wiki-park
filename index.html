wiki park

Before everything was a search away, you learnt things as they came: from whoever you met, at whatever pace the day allowed.

Wiki Park is a shared park of Wikipedia articles, where nature, technology and being human meet. There's no search, no zoom and no map. You see only what's around you, the way you would on a walk, and other people stroll here with you.

how to walk

  • Scroll, drag or use the arrow keys. Push harder, or hold Shift, to jog for a moment.
  • Click a plant to read it. Flowers are short articles, trees the long ones. Its colour tells you what it's about:
  • nature
  • technology
  • being human
  • Found something you liked? Press L, or the lantern on its card, to leave a lantern for others. Press L again to take it back. The pointer in the corner leads to the nearest one.
  • Double-click open ground to plant an article of your own, one a day.
  • The park keeps your local time. At night you see only what your lantern lights.
  • Pale arrows are other walkers. A tiny dot beside a plant means someone planted it.
  • Not everything here is an article.
